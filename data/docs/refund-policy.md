# Refund Policy

_Last updated: 2024-01-02_

This is sample knowledge-base content bundled with the MCP Connect starter,
used to demonstrate the docs/knowledge-base connector. Replace this folder
with your own internal docs, wiki export, or Confluence/Notion dump.

## Standard refunds

- Customers may request a full refund within **30 days** of purchase for
  any plan, no questions asked.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business
  days of approval.
- Annual plans cancelled after the 30-day window are refunded on a
  pro-rated basis for unused months, minus any volume discount already
  applied.

## Non-refundable items

- Custom onboarding or professional services engagements are non-refundable
  once work has started.
- Usage-based overage charges (e.g. extra API calls) are not refundable.

## How to process a refund

1. Confirm the request is within policy using the account's `created_at`
   and billing history.
2. File a ticket with the "billing" tag and tag `@billing-team`.
3. Approved refunds over $10,000 require a second approver.

## Escalations

For anything outside this policy (partial-year enterprise contracts,
disputed charges, etc.), escalate to the Finance team rather than approving
directly.
